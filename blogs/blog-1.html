How to Choose a Trusted WHO-GMP Generic Medicine Supplier from India

Every global buyer needs a reliable sourcing partner. This guide explains the standards, documents, and supplier qualities that matter most for Indian pharmaceutical exports.

Published: 4 June 2026 • Category: Supplier Selection

India is one of the world’s largest producers of generic medicines. For international procurement, buyers must go beyond price and look for WHO-GMP quality, proper export documentation, and transparent supplier processes.

1. Confirm WHO-GMP and regulatory credentials

WHO-GMP certification is a key indicator of manufacturing quality. Choose suppliers who clearly share their WHO-GMP certificates, drug license details, and export compliance records.

  • Verify WHO-GMP status directly with the manufacturer.
  • Ask for drug license copies and IEC/RCMC registration.
  • Request batch release documentation for export orders.

2. Evaluate product quality and testing

Reliable suppliers back medicines with quality testing and batch traceability. Ask for COAs (Certificate of Analysis), stability data, and a clear description of packaging and shelf life.

A transparent supplier will explain how they maintain quality for antibiotics, injectables, OTC products, and other formulations.

3. Assess export experience and global shipping capability

Exporting pharmaceuticals requires familiarity with international logistics, customs rules, and destination-specific paperwork. Suppliers working with Africa, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Latin America are more likely to avoid delays.

4. Confirm packaging, labeling, and shelf-life readiness

Proper export packaging protects medicines during long transit. Buyers should verify the supplier uses secure cartons, moisture-resistant seals, and accurate labels that meet the destination market requirements.

5. Prefer open communication and responsive support

Strong supplier relationships depend on communication. Choose a partner that answers questions promptly, provides shipment tracking updates, and can support local registration or documentation requests when needed.
