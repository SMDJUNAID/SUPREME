5 Pharmaceutical Export Compliance Tips for Smooth International Shipments

Follow these practical steps to keep your medicine orders moving quickly and avoid customs issues when sourcing from India.

Published: 4 June 2026 • Category: Compliance

Exporting pharmaceuticals is different from shipping ordinary goods. The right paperwork, packaging, and supplier checks can prevent delays and keep your product reliable at every destination.

1. Prepare complete export documentation

Make sure your purchase orders include all required certificates: commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and COA. For medicines, also request the manufacturer’s WHO-GMP certificate, batch release certificate, and any supporting test reports.

2. Choose WHO-GMP certified suppliers

Using a WHO-GMP certified supplier reduces regulatory risk. It also gives customs agents and import authorities confidence that the medicines meet international production standards.

3. Use secure, export-ready packaging

  • Select solid cartons and protective inner packaging for tablets and capsules.
  • Use temperature-controlled packaging for sensitive injectables when needed.
  • Ensure labeling is complete, clear, and compliant with destination requirements.

4. Know the destination market requirements

Different countries have different registration, labelling, and customs rules. Ask your supplier for destination-specific packaging instructions or any product registration support you may need.

5. Stay connected through transit

Track shipments from dispatch to delivery. Ask the exporter for GPS or airway bill updates and confirm that the logistics partner can handle pharmaceutical shipments responsibly.
