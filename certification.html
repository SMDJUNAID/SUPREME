Licenses & Certifications

Every certification we hold reflects our commitment to quality, safety, and international compliance.

Drug License (20B & 21B)

Authorized license for wholesale distribution of pharmaceutical products as per Indian drug regulations. This ensures all our products meet domestic and international safety standards.

Verified & Active

Import Export Code (IEC)

Official certification issued by DGFT allowing international trade and export of pharmaceutical products. A mandatory requirement for all legitimate pharmaceutical exporters in India.

Verified & Active

RCMC Certificate

Registration Cum Membership Certificate enabling export promotion and global trade compliance. Issued by the Pharmaceuticals Export Promotion Council of India (Pharmexcil).

Verified & Active

WHO-GMP Certification

Ensures that our partner manufacturers follow strict Good Manufacturing Practices as per WHO standards. This is the gold standard for pharmaceutical quality assurance worldwide.

Verified & Active

ISO Certification

Demonstrates commitment to quality management systems and consistent product standards. Ensures that every process — from sourcing to delivery — meets internationally recognized benchmarks.
