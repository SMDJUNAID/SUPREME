## **Content SEO Checklist (For Each Blog Post)**

- [ ] **Title**: Include main keyword, 50-60 characters
  - Example: "How to Choose a Trusted WHO-GMP Medicine Supplier from India"
  
- [ ] **Meta Description**: 150-160 characters, include CTA
  - Example: "Learn how to verify WHO-GMP certification, quality standards, and regulatory compliance when selecting pharmaceutical suppliers from India. Expert guide for international buyers."

- [ ] **URL Slug**: Short, descriptive, with hyphens
  - Example: `/blogs/how-to-choose-medicine-supplier-india.html`

- [ ] **Headings (H2, H3)**: Include keywords naturally
  - H1: Only one per page (main title)
  - H2: 3-5 per article
  - H3: Under each H2

- [ ] **Word Count**: 1500+ words for good ranking

- [ ] **Keywords**: Main keyword 2-3 times, variations throughout
  
- [ ] **Internal Links**: Link to 3-5 other relevant pages
  - Example: Link to Products page, About us, Contact page

- [ ] **Images**: Add 2-3 relevant images
  - Use descriptive alt text: "WHO-GMP certification logo for pharmaceutical exports"
  - Compress images to <100KB each

- [ ] **Call-to-Action**: End with clear CTA
  - "Ready to source medicines from India? Contact us for quotations"
  - "Get in touch with Supreme Medical Agency for bulk orders"

- [ ] **Conclusion**: Summary + contact invitation

## **Step-by-Step: Publishing a Blog Post**

### **1. Research (30 minutes)**
- Keyword research: What do buyers search for?
- Competitor analysis: What's already ranking?
- Outline: Main points to cover

### **2. Writing (1-2 hours)**
- Write 1500+ words on topic
- Use natural keywords (2-3 times)
- Clear headings (H1, H2, H3)
- Add 2-3 images with alt text
- Internal links to your pages
- Strong CTA at end

### **3. SEO Optimization (30 minutes)**
- Title: Include keyword (50-60 chars)
- Meta description (150-160 chars)
- URL slug (short & descriptive)
- H1, H2, H3 structure verified
- Links checked

### **4. Submit to Google (5 minutes)**
- Go to Google Search Console
- Submit new URL
- Wait 24-48 hours for crawl
